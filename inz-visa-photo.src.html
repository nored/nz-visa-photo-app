Chain of custody

Pipeline: your camera → 3:4 crop → JPEG encode. No brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness, background replacement, or skin-smoothing changes at any step.

Auto-filter warning. If your phone or laptop applies beautification or filters automatically, turn them off before you start — INZ rejects filtered photos.

Verify manually. Machine checks help but do not replace the INZ checklist: neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open, hair not covering ears, no tinted glasses, no head coverings unless religious/medical.
